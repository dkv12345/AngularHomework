How this works (Exercise 14 Implementation):

  • Data structure defined in CatalogService containing categories with nested Products array.
  • Outer loop *ngFor="let cat of categories" renders each category block with its ID and Name.
  • Inner loop *ngFor="let p of cat.Products" renders the corresponding product rows inside each category's table.
