How this works (Exercise 18 Implementation):

  • Data is stored in assets/data/customers.json conforming to the structured customer type model.
  • CustomerService injects Angular HttpClient to perform asynchronous GET requests with RxJS Observable streams and error fallback.
  • Angular template uses nested <tbody *ngFor="let group of filteredGroups"> to render distinct group header rows followed by individual customer records with high-resolution avatar rendering.
